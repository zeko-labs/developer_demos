Operator Panel

        

          

            Live operator controls for the on-chain settlement anchor. Use this to inspect zkApp roots, inspect the current Merkle-backed private state, build the next private-state witness, and prove the next pending batch.
          

          

            Manual operator actions are gated by the operator admin key. The background operator process still runs independently.
          

          

            
            
          

          

            
            
            
            
            
          

          

          

        

      

    


    

      

        

          
Funding Guide

          

            

              1) Connect Wallet.
              2) Acquire testnet tETH/tZEKO/tMINA in wallet.
              3) Mint and Redeem privacy notes in Funding UI.
              4) Place orders in Trading UI.
            

          

        

        

          
Privacy Notes

          

            

              Convert an on-chain vault deposit into private collateral. Trading uses your available notes automatically unless you override with specific note hashes.
            

            

              

              

              

            

            

            

              Step 1: Click Deposit Funds & Mint Notes and approve in wallet.

              Step 2: Your private collateral appears below and is auto-used by trading.

              Fallback mint controls remain in Advanced.
